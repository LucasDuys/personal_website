# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, founders, investors, and technical collaborators evaluating Lucas Duys through his work, trajectory, and evidence of execution.

## Product Purpose

Lucasduys.com is a living visual CV. It should make Lucas's technical depth, founder trajectory, and ability to ship ambitious work legible within minutes, then give interested visitors a direct path to his work, professional profiles, or email.

## Positioning

The portfolio connects engineering outcomes, startup work, and real public milestones into one evidence-backed story. It shows the work and the context in which it was built instead of relying on a generic skills list or self-description.

## Operating Context

Most visitors will arrive from GitHub, LinkedIn, an application, or a direct introduction. They may scan quickly on mobile or review the full history on desktop. The site is a single-page CV with anchored sections and external links to projects and profiles.

- Keep the site statically exportable with Next.js.

- Present quantified achievements with context and restraint.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is Lucas Duys.
- Voice is direct, ambitious, technically specific, and confident without hype.
- Real images and real outcomes carry the story.
- The site should feel fascinating and memorable while remaining usable as a CV.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before adjectives.
2. A visitor should understand the trajectory before reading every detail.
3. Numbers earn attention only when their context is clear.
4. The work and photography lead; interface chrome recedes.
5. Memorable motion must never make scrolling feel controlled or delayed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor reduced-motion preferences, preserve semantic navigation and keyboard focus, keep content visible without animation, and maintain WCAG AA contrast across supported viewports.

- Each project and each claim appears on the page exactly once; the film carries the headline figures and the record carries the detail.
